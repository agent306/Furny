## Deviations / decisions (please review)
1. FurnitureRevision immutability is "like PlanRevision" except the BOM result columns (`bom, price, bom_status, engine_version, catalog_version`) may be updated: 03 §3.5 and 08 §9.1/§6 require `ComputeRevisionBom`/`RefreshRevisionBom` to write them after creation, and 02 §5.2 says `bom` is null until the job finishes. Updating any other column (document, reason, number, author, change_summary, ...) throws LogicException; unchanged saves are fine. Tested both ways.
2. Revision relation is named `furnitureDesign()` (not `design()`) so `FurnitureRevision::factory()->for($design)` and the default FK resolve.
3. BlueprintImport `#[Fillable]` only has upload description + hints (`user_id, mode, original_filename, mime_type, file_size_bytes, selected_page, hints`); status, plan_id, source_kind, page_count, ai_*, credits, results, errors, attempts and stamps are action-written (avoids the lifecycle-in-Fillable minor from Task 5). Same stance for FurnitureDesign (no owner_id/working_version/current_revision_id/locked_by_order_id).
4. `source` collection accepts the declared MIME list of 06 §2.1 (including `application/octet-stream`/`text/plain` for DXF/HEIC) since sniffing is done earlier by ProcessUpload; flagged in case P6 wants it relaxed/tightened.
5. Not built (fixtures/dependencies do not exist yet): factory `fromFixture`, `invalid()`, `lockedBy($order)`, import `pages`/`normalized` media states and vision fixtures (P2/P5/P6 per brief and ruling on fixtures).
6. `ledgerEntries()`/`usageEvents()` relations of 06 §4.2 and `stage()` are omitted (AI models and ImportStage do not exist yet).
